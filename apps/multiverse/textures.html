Pattern tiles: textures.js calculates these at startup -- one exact repeat of each building pattern, baked with mipmaps and anisotropic filtering. Left: the stored channels (R brightness / 1.5, G accent, B dark figure; grass: blade cover, its tip-place and tone, both premultiplied). Middle: applied to a sample stone and accent colour. Right: the tile repeated 4 x 4, as it tiles on a wall. build/textures.py saves the same images into texture/ for inspection; the game never loads them.
